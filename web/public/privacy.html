Privacy summary (pre-release)

This is a draft for engineering review, not a finalized legal privacy notice. Devcon Follow is an independent attendee networking product.

What we collect

Account identity from your chosen sign-in provider; an opaque organizer-attested ticket identifier, not your entry code; optional name, roles, interests, profile photo and social links; follows; your private direct messages and public-landmark meetup invitations; and location-sharing consent. When you explicitly share location, a short-lived latest GPS reading is retained for proximity and approved navigation. The app does not intentionally maintain a historical GPS trail.

Who can see what

Discoverable profiles are shown to eligible participants, including explicitly labeled community participants whose Devcon ticket has not been verified. Exact GPS coordinates are revealed only through recipient-specific, expiring consent. General radar shows coarse distance bands and freshness, not precise map coordinates. Direct messages are accessible to both participants and authorized server operators for abuse investigation; they are not end-to-end encrypted. User roles are self-declared, not professional endorsements.

Control and deletion

Location sharing is off by default and expires automatically. You may immediately revoke sharing, block other attendees, hide your profile and request account deletion within the app. Your account-deletion action removes posts, comments, likes and messages associated with your account; abuse reports and minimal audit records may be retained for limited security purposes under a finalized retention policy. Invitation codes and invited email addresses are stored only as keyed digests; redeemed invitations contain the account subject and expire. Invitation records are removed seven days after their final expiry.

Operational requirements before launch

A privacy owner must publish the retention schedule, identity-provider and infrastructure subprocessors, support contact, lawful basis, venue dates, age restrictions, data-export process and incident-response contact before opening registration.
